Who is Local Pass for?

Tourists, digital nomads and locals who want trusted Belgrade places with useful perks.

Do partners need a separate app?

Not for the MVP. A simple validation page is enough to confirm an active pass.

Are the perks vague discounts?

No. Every perk should say where it works, when it works and what the user gets.
