Svet Sauna Banjica

A spacious sauna and wellness center inside SC Banjica, built for heat rituals, cold water recovery, pools, massage and slow relaxation.

Call spa center Open map

Why go

A complete sauna circuit with heat, steam, cold water and pool access in one place.

Svet Sauna Banjica combines several sauna experiences with jacuzzi, frigidarium, quiet rest areas, massage options and access to the SC Banjica pools. It is a practical choice for recovery after training, a slower weekend reset or a simple wellness session without leaving the Banjica area.

01

Finnish saunas

Dry heat rooms for classic sauna sessions and deep warming.

02

Russian and Turkish sauna

Different heat and humidity profiles for a softer, steam-forward ritual.

03

Jacuzzi and frigidarium

Hydrotherapy and cold water contrast after sauna sessions.

04

Pool and massage

Access to SC Banjica pools and massage options for a longer recovery visit.
