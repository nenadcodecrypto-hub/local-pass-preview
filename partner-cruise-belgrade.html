Experience · Sava & Danube

Cruise Belgrade

Sightseeing cruises through Belgrade’s rivers, with routes past the Sava, Danube, Zemun, Gardoš Tower, Kalemegdan and the city bridges.

Call cruise team Open map

Why go

See Belgrade from the water, where the Sava and Danube change the whole shape of the city.

Cruise Belgrade runs daytime, sunset and night river tours with relaxed music, guided sightseeing and routes that pass the confluence, New Belgrade, Zemun and the bridges. It is an easy experience for visitors who want photos, views and a softer pace than another walk through the city center.
